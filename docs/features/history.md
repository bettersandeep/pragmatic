## Version history (time machine)

Every change to a board or doc is kept; nothing is overwritten.

In the app (clock icon, or the doc menu):
- A list of versions: one per editing session (a pause of more than 5 minutes starts a new one), with the people who edited.
- Preview a version on the board.
- Restore it: a new change that everyone sees, so the restore can be undone too.
- Fork it into a new board or doc.

How long history is kept:
- Every change for 30 days.
- One point per hour for 6 months.
- One point per day after that, for ever.

Claude's edits appear in the history as "<name> (Claude)".

Backups of the database itself: copy the data folder (built-in database), or use your Postgres server's backups.
