## Collaboration

Live editing:
- Everyone on a board or doc sees changes at once, with no save button.
- Cursors and selections of the other people, with their names.
- Text boxes, task titles and descriptions, mind map nodes and table cells are shared text: two people typing in one box keep both edits.
- A colored outline shows who is typing where.

Comments:
- Pins on a point or on an object, threads with replies, @mentions, reactions, resolve, edit and delete your own.
- The server writes comments with the signed-in person as author.

Inbox:
- Mentions (in comments, and in board text such as stickies and mind map nodes), replies in your threads, comments on cards you watch, card assignments, and shares with you.
- A mention in board text notifies once a day per person and object.
- Nobody is told about their own actions or about boards they cannot open.
- Mark one or all as read.

Sharing (Share button):
- Private items: only the owner, plus people the owner adds.
- Team items: everyone in the workspace can edit.
- Per person: edit, comment or view.
- Link access for anyone in the workspace who has the link: none (default), view, comment or edit.
- Optional: also share a view-only copy in your Google Drive.
- The server checks the role on every request, and it drops board changes from viewers and commenters.

Not done:
- Permissions per folder (sharing is per board or doc).
